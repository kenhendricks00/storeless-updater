# Storeless Updater preliminary name screen

Screen date: 2026-07-27

Scope: downloadable Windows software, primarily United States launch use, with a basic international and multi-surface screen. Likely relevant categories include downloadable software and hosted software services (Nice Classes 9 and 42), although class numbers alone do not decide trademark conflicts.

## Recommendation

Use **Storeless Updater** as the project name and **Unofficial Storeless Updater for ChatGPT on Windows** as the descriptive subtitle. The name communicates that the Microsoft Store client is unnecessary without using OpenAI's trademarks as the project's own brand.

This is a preliminary knock-out screen, not legal clearance. No search can guarantee that a name is available, and a trademark attorney should review the final name and logo before significant commercial use.

## Other generated candidates

They were rejected or deprioritized because they were less accurate, more crowded, more technical than the user-facing job, or more metaphorical than necessary.

## Legal boundary

The preliminary screen found no obvious relevant exact conflict for Storeless Updater in the sources checked on 2026-07-27. The phrase is descriptive and therefore may be difficult to protect as an exclusive mark. A comprehensive clearance may require direct similarity searches in USPTO Trademark Search, TMview, WIPO's Global Brand Database, state trademark and business-entity registries, deeper common-law marketplace searches, review in every intended launch country, and separate review of the final logo.
